# 🛵 Swiggy Sales Performance Deep Dive

A comprehensive sales analysis of Swiggy's order and delivery data to uncover revenue drivers, demand patterns, and growth opportunities across city tiers.

---

## 📌 Project Objective

To analyse Swiggy's sales performance and answer key business questions:
- Where is revenue concentrated, and which areas are underperforming?
- When are peak demand windows, and how can they be leveraged?
- How do customers behave across different city tiers?
- What actionable recommendations can improve GMV and order frequency?

## 🔍 Key Analysis Areas

### 1. Revenue & GMV Analysis
- Identified top revenue-generating cities and categories
- Analysed GMV trends over time to spot growth and decline periods

### 2. Demand Pattern Analysis
- Mapped peak order windows by time of day, day of week, and season
- Identified underperforming categories with low order frequency

### 3. City Tier Segmentation
- Segmented performance across Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 cities
- Found that **15% of revenue is concentrated in 3 city clusters**, informing targeted expansion strategy

### 4. Customer Cohort Behaviour
- Tracked order frequency and repeat purchase behaviour by customer cohort
- Built a dashboard tracking GMV, order frequency, and cohort retention

## 💡 Key Findings & Recommendations

| Finding | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| 15% revenue concentrated in 3 city clusters | Prioritise marketing spend and delivery infrastructure in these clusters |
| Peak demand on weekends between 7–10 PM | Optimise restaurant partner availability and delivery fleet during these windows |
| Underperforming categories in Tier 2 cities | Run targeted promotions to grow category awareness |

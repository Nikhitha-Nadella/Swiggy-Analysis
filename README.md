# Swiggy-Analysis
Portfolio Project Case Study: Swiggy Sales &amp; Performance Deep Dive

Swiggy Sales & Performance Deep Dive (Excel-Based Analysis)A comprehensive data analysis project examining transaction and rating data from a major food delivery platform (Swiggy) to uncover key sales drivers, regional performance trends, and opportunities for operational improvement.This project specifically demonstrates proficiency in handling, cleaning, and aggregating large datasets ($\sim200,000$ records) using advanced features within Microsoft Excel.

The primary objective was to translate raw sales data into actionable business strategies for optimizing revenue and customer satisfaction.

KEY METRICS:

Total Orders,"197,430",High operational volume forms a strong basis for time-series and geographical analysis.
Total Sales (GMV),∼$53 Million INR,Provides the baseline for calculating regional and category contributions.
Average Rating,4.34/5.00,"Indicates generally positive customer satisfaction, allowing focus on efficiency."
Average Order Value (AOV),∼268.51 INR,The key metric for identifying high-revenue cuisine categories for promotional focus.

The entire analysis was performed using Microsoft Excel, demonstrating mastery of complex spreadsheet functions and data organization.

The final dashboard visually summarizes the key findings, including the regional breakdown of sales and the contribution of top-performing categories.

Future Analysis & Strategic Roadmap (Next Steps)
This section highlights strategic thinking by outlining high-impact analyses that would be performed with additional data. This demonstrates an understanding of how data analysis translates into operational strategy.

Focus 1: Customer Loyalty (Swiggy One):

Goal: Quantify the True Lifetime Value (LTV) of members vs. non-members.

Analysis: Compare AOV, order frequency, and discount dependency between membership groups to justify investment in the loyalty program.

Focus 2: Operational Efficiency (Delivery Time):

Goal: Isolate the impact of logistics on customer satisfaction.

Analysis: Correlate Actual Delivery Time with Rating to determine the penalty for late deliveries, identifying specific city/restaurant bottlenecks for operational improvements.
